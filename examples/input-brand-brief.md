# Example Brand Brief — Napwell Mattresses

## Required Fields
- **Brand name:** Napwell
- **Product:** Organic latex mattress with cooling gel layer
- **Target audience:** Sleep-deprived professionals, 30-45, $75k+ income, urban, work from home or hybrid, experience back pain and anxiety-related insomnia
- **Key benefit:** Fall asleep in under 10 minutes, stay asleep all night
- **Tone of voice:** Confident but empathetic. Like a friend who has their life together but remembers what it's like not to.
- **Format:** Landing page

## Optional
- **Constraints:** Must mention 100-night trial. No medical claims.
- **Competitive context:** Vs. Casper, Purple, Tempur-Pedic. Differentiator: organic materials + cooling tech.
- **Existing brand voice:** "We don't sell mattresses. We sell mornings where you don't hate your alarm."
- **Campaign history:** Previous campaign: "Your bed shouldn't be the hardest part of your day." Performed well with 30-35 demographic.
