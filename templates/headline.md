# Headline Template — Bly's 4U Formula

## Checklist Before Writing
- [ ] Urgent — Why now? Deadline, scarcity, pain point.
- [ ] Unique — Hasn't been said this way before.
- [ ] Ultra-specific — Numbers, names, exact outcomes.
- [ ] Useful — Clear benefit to the reader.
- [ ] Active voice only.
- [ ] ≤ 12 words (print) or ≤ 10 words (digital) or ≤ 7 words (billboard).

## Structure Options

### 1. How-to
"How to [achieve result] in [timeframe] without [common pain]"

### 2. List
"[Number] ways to [result] that [surprising qualifier]"

### 3. Question
"What if [provocative scenario]?"

### 4. Command
"[Verb] your [noun] and [result]"

### 5. News
"Finally: [product] that [specific benefit]"

### 6. Testimonial
"'[Quote]' — [Name], [credential]"

### 7. Direct
"[Benefit] for [audience] who [qualifier]"

## Examples of Excellence
- "Lemonade. 90 seconds. $5/month." (Lemonade Insurance)
- "They laughed when I sat down at the piano." (U.S. School of Music)
- "The man in the Hathaway shirt." (Hathaway)
- "Think small." (Volkswagen)
- "We try harder." (Avis)
- "Just do it." (Nike)
