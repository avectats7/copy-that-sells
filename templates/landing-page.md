# Landing Page Template — Before/After/Bridge + Social Proof

## Structure

### 1. Headline
Promise the transformation. Lead with the benefit.

### 2. Subhead
Specific proof or mechanism. How does it work?

### 3. Before
Acknowledge the pain. Make it felt.
"Tired of..." / "Sick of..." / "Frustrated by..."

### 4. After
Paint the picture. Make them want it.
"Imagine..." / "Picture..." / "What if..."

### 5. Bridge
How you get there. Product as vehicle.
"[Product] does [mechanism] so you can [outcome]."

### 6. Social Proof
3 testimonials minimum. Photos, names, specific results.
Format: "'[Quote]' — [Name], [credential], [specific result]"

### 7. CTA
One action. One button. No choices = no paralysis.
Action verb + specific outcome.
"Get the template" > "Click here"
"Start my free trial" > "Submit"

## Rules
- No paragraph over 3 sentences in the hero section.
- Every claim must have proof or be immediately believable.
- CTA above the fold. CTA at the bottom. One in the middle if long.
- No exclamation marks in premium/luxury.
- White space is your friend.
