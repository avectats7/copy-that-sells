# Manifesto Template

## The Belief Structure
A manifesto is not a sales pitch. It's a statement of belief that makes the reader want to join your tribe.

## Rules
1. Start with "We believe..." or "Because..."
2. No product mention until paragraph 3.
3. 3-5 short paragraphs.
4. End with a call to belief, not a call to action.
5. Must pass the "tear test" — does it give you goosebumps?
6. Use repetition for rhythm.
7. Short sentences. Punchy. Poetic but not pretentious.

## Structure

### Paragraph 1: The Belief
"We believe [something fundamental about the world]."

### Paragraph 2: The Problem
"But [what's wrong with the current state]."

### Paragraph 3: The Shift
"So [what you're doing differently]."

### Paragraph 4: The Invitation
"Join us. [Why this matters to the reader]."

### Paragraph 5: The Closing
"[One sentence that sums it all up]."

## Examples of Excellence

### Apple — Think Different (1997)
"Here's to the crazy ones. The misfits. The rebels. The troublemakers. The round pegs in the square holes. The ones who see things differently."

### Nike — Find Your Greatness (2012)
"Greatness is not some rare DNA strand. It's not some precious thing. Greatness is no more unique to us than breathing. We're all capable of it. All of us."
