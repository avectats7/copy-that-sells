# Tagline Template

## The 5-7 Word Rule
A tagline must be:
- Ownable (not generic)
- Memorable (rhythm, rhyme, or surprise)
- Timeless (works in 10 years)
- Work without the logo
- Contain a verb or implied action

## Structure Options

### 1. Command
"[Verb] [object]." — Direct, strong.

### 2. Promise
"[Benefit] [guarantee]." — Reassuring.

### 3. Truth
"[Noun] [is] [adjective]." — Declarative, confident.

### 4. Contrast
"[X], not [Y]." — Memorable, defining.

### 5. Rhyme
"[Word] [word that rhymes]." — Musical, sticky.

## Examples of Excellence
- "Just Do It." — Nike (3 words, command, universal)
- "Think Different." — Apple (2 words, command, provocative)
- "The Ultimate Driving Machine." — BMW (4 words, superlative)
- "Melts in Your Mouth, Not in Your Hands." — M&Ms (contrast, rhyme)
- "Diamonds Are Forever." — De Beers (4 words, emotional)
- "We Try Harder." — Avis (3 words, underdog, honest)
