## Long Copy Openings

### Ogilvy — Rolls-Royce
"At 60 miles an hour, the loudest noise in this new Rolls-Royce comes from the electric clock."
- **Why it works:** Specific, surprising, credible. You can hear the clock.

### Gary Halbert — Coat of Arms
"If you have ever bought anything by mail, your name is on a mailing list. And if your name is on a mailing list, somebody is selling it."
- **Why it works:** Universal truth, personal, builds tension.

### Eugene Schwartz — Eye-See
"Do your eyes ache after reading? Do words blur? Do you get headaches?"
- **Why it works:** Questions that diagnose. The reader answers yes, yes, yes.

### Claude Hopkins — Schlitz Beer
"We carry the beer through a plate glass room. Then it is filtered. Then it is sterilized."
- **Why it works:** Specific process. Boring details become fascinating when they prove quality.

## Emails

### Subject Lines That Work
- "I was wrong about [topic]" — curiosity + humility
- "Your [item] is waiting" — personalization + urgency
- "[Name], quick question" — personal + low commitment
- "The [number] [things] I wish I knew at [age]" — regret + list
- "This changes everything" — curiosity (but only if true)

### Email Openings
- "I made a mistake yesterday." — vulnerability, hook
- "Three years ago, I couldn't afford groceries." — story, relatability
- "You asked. We listened." — customer-centric, responsive

## Landing Pages

### Before/After/Bridge Examples

**Before:** "Tired of spending hours on payroll every week?"
**After:** "Imagine finishing payroll in 10 minutes and actually enjoying your Friday."
**Bridge:** "Gusto automates the boring stuff so you can get back to building your business."

**Before:** "Sick of email apps that don't talk to each other?"
**After:** "Picture one inbox where every message, from every account, lives in perfect harmony."
**Bridge:** "Superhuman unifies your Gmail, Outlook, and custom accounts in one blazing-fast interface."

## Manifestos

### Apple — "Think Different" (1997)
"Here's to the crazy ones. The misfits. The rebels. The troublemakers. The round pegs in the square holes. The ones who see things differently."
- **Why it works:** No product mention for 5 sentences. Builds belief first. Uses "the" + noun pattern for rhythm.

### Nike — "Find Your Greatness" (2012)
"Greatness is not some rare DNA strand. It's not some precious thing. Greatness is no more unique to us than breathing. We're all capable of it. All of us."
- **Why it works:** Demolishes the excuse ("I'm not gifted") before offering the solution.

## Product Descriptions

### Feature → Benefit → Outcome
**Feature:** "100% organic cotton"
**Benefit:** "Breathable and soft against your skin"
**Outcome:** "Sleep through the night without waking up sweaty"

**Feature:** "Noise-canceling technology"
**Benefit:** "Blocks 95% of ambient sound"
**Outcome:** "Hear every note of your music, not the guy coughing on the train"

## What Makes These Work

1. **Specificity** — Numbers, names, exact details.
2. **Surprise** — The reader didn't expect this angle.
3. **Credibility** — Proof, process, or a specific claim.
4. **Emotion** — Fear, desire, pride, regret, hope.
5. **Rhythm** — Short sentences. Varied length. Read it aloud.
6. **One Idea** — Every piece has a single, clear focus.
