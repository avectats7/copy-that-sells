Using Before/After/Bridge because the audience is already aware of their pain (insomnia is a daily struggle) but hasn't found a solution they trust. The empathetic tone acknowledges the frustration without pity. The 100-night trial is the risk reversal that closes the gap between interest and action.

## Recommended: Option A

Option A is the strongest because:
1. **Universal pain point:** Everyone who can't sleep knows the "brain starts its nightly TED Talk" feeling. It's specific, relatable, and slightly funny.
2. **No competitor bashing:** Option B risks alienating readers who liked their Casper. Option A focuses on the user's pain, not the competition's failure.
3. **Stronger social proof:** Option A's testimonials are more emotional and specific. "First time in 10 years" hits harder than "best $1,200 I've ever spent."
4. **Empathy without weakness:** The tone is confident ("your body can do what it's supposed to do") but acknowledges the struggle. Option C's founder story is compelling but makes the brand about one person, not the customer.
