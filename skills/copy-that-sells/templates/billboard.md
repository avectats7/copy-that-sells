# Billboard / OOH Template

## The 3-Second Rule
A billboard reader is driving 60mph. You have 3 seconds. One image + one line = one idea.

## Constraints
- ≤ 7 words maximum. Ideally 5.
- One visual concept.
- No fine print.
- No URLs unless absolutely necessary (and short).
- Must work as a sentence AND as a visual concept.

## Structure Options

### 1. Command
"[Verb] [noun]." — 2-3 words, action-oriented.

### 2. Statement
"[Subject] [verb]." — 2-3 words, declarative.

### 3. Question
"[Question]?" — Provocative, short.

### 4. Fragment
"[Noun phrase]." — Intriguing, incomplete.

## Examples of Excellence
- "Hello Boys." — Wonderbra (2 words, direct, provocative)
- "Think Big." — IMAX (2 words, command, relevant)
- "Got Milk?" — Dairy Board (2 words, question, iconic)
- "Just Do It." — Nike (3 words, command, universal)
- "Think Different." — Apple (2 words, command, provocative)
