# Email Template — PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solve)

## Structure

### Subject Line
- Curiosity or benefit. ≤ 8 words.
- No emojis in B2B.
- No ALL CAPS.
- One idea. No compound sentences.

### Opening (Problem)
State the problem in one sentence. Make it felt.
Not: "Many people struggle with sleep."
Yes: "You wake up at 3am and stare at the ceiling until the alarm."

### Agitate
"And it's getting worse because..."
2-3 sentences max. Make the pain sharper.

### Solve
Introduce the product as the logical solution.
Not: "Our mattress uses advanced foam technology."
Yes: "Napwell is the only mattress designed by insomniacs."

### Proof
One specific result. One number.
"Sarah from Austin fell asleep in 8 minutes on night 3."

### CTA
Single link. Action verb.
"Get the template" / "Read the case study" / "Book a call"

## Rules
- Subject line must work as a standalone sentence.
- Opening sentence must be under 15 words.
- No paragraph over 3 sentences.
- One CTA only. Multiple CTAs = no action.
- Sign-off must feel human, not corporate.
